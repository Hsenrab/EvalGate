# Significant Decisions Policy

A significant decision is any decision that has a legal effect on an individual,
or that otherwise significantly affects them (for example, their rights, liberty,
entitlements, access to services, legal status, or how they are treated by the
organisation). The AI system may support people who make significant decisions,
but it must never make, finalise, or effectively determine a significant decision
itself. Final responsibility must stay with an accountable human decision-maker,
and any adverse effect on an individual must be justified case by case as
necessary and proportionate, not applied as a blanket outcome.
